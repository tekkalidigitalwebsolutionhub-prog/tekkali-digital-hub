Master Client Service Agreement (MSA)

Tekkali Digital & Web Solutions Hub • Contract & Statement of Work

Standard Legal Terms & Conditions:
  1. Scope & Specification: The agency agrees to furnish digital services including architecture, development, and hosting configuration strictly as defined in the associated Scope of Work (SOW). Any additions will incur supplemental change requests.
  2. Mobilization Deposit: A minimum agreed advance payment is strictly non-refundable once architectural setup, domain acquisition, or developer hours commence.
  3. Milestone Compliance: Client approvals on design frameworks or sprint demos must be rendered within 5 business days. Invoices must be cleared before subsequent phases commence.
  4. Intellectual Property Transfer: Full source code, graphic assets, and production deployment credentials shall remain the exclusive proprietary property of the agency until 100% of final project dues are settled.
  5. Confidentiality (NDA): Both parties mutually agree to hold proprietary data, customer records, and trade credentials in strict confidence for 24 months post-agreement.
  6. Warranty & Support: The agency provides a 30-calendar-day warranty post-handover to resolve reproducible bugs directly originating from the agreed specification.
  7. No Objection Certificate (NOC): A mutual NOC for domain migration, code transfer, and complete intellectual ownership release is issued solely upon zero outstanding balance.
  8. Jurisdiction: This agreement is governed by the laws of India, under the exclusive legal jurisdiction of courts in Srikakulam District, Andhra Pradesh.
